Extract every obligation in the supplied documents: each thing a named party
must do.

For each one, record who owes it, what they must do, and -- exactly as the
document words it -- the period and the event the period runs from.

Do not calculate dates. Record "thirty (30) days" as written and name the trigger
event ("receipt of invoice", "the effective date"). Date arithmetic is performed
outside this step, and a date you compute here would bypass that check.

Include only obligations the document actually imposes. A right ("may terminate")
is not an obligation. A recital is not an obligation.
