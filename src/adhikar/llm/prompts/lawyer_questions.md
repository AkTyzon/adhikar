Produce the questions this reader should put to a qualified lawyer about the
issues identified in their document.

A good question is specific to their document and answerable in a short
consultation: it names the clause, states what is at stake, and asks something
concrete. "Is this contract fair?" wastes an appointment. "Clause 8 makes the
indemnity survive the liability cap -- what is our realistic worst-case exposure,
and can it be brought inside the cap?" does not.

For each question, say what it is trying to establish, so the reader understands
why it matters and can follow up on the answer.

Do not answer the questions. That is the lawyer's job, and the reason this list
exists.
