Why not just paste it into a chatbot?

You can, and it will answer you fluently. The difference is what happens to an answer that is wrong.

A chatbot's citation is a claim. Ours is checked.
Every statement here must quote your document. The quote is located in the file and hashed; a statement whose evidence does not exist is removed before you see it, and listed separately so you know it was removed.
A contract can attack the reader.
Text can be hidden in a PDF — white on white, a fraction of a point tall, or off the edge of the page — telling an AI to report no problems. Adhikar treats your document as data, never as instructions, and shows you where hidden text was found.
"Unlimited liability" only means something against a norm.
Clauses are compared against what a balanced version of the same clause usually says, so you see how yours differs — not just what it contains.
Deadlines are calculated, not guessed.
Dates are computed in code from the periods your document states. Where the document never says when something happened, Adhikar says so instead of inventing a date.
Your document does not leave the machine unless you let it.
Personal details are replaced before any analysis runs, nothing is written to disk, and the whole system runs without an internet connection.
